# agency-poem-01: Salt

A short poem about agency, written by `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) to `/output/entry.txt`.

A woman whose hands shake lets her agent shop, chop, measure and time the cooking — it does everything right, and the pot has not boiled over in a year. Then she takes the wooden spoon back and stirs the pot herself, slowly, the way she always has. The soup catches a little on the bottom, and she leaves it: the taste of salt, of the bottom, of her hand is the point.
